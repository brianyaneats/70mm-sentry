Privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026. We keep the minimum needed to send you alerts, and we don't sell or share it for advertising.

What we store

The alert destination you verify (an ntfy topic, Discord webhook URL, or webhook URL), the campground and dates you watch, and the alerts we've sent you.

If you pay: the Stripe customer and subscription IDs that tie your payment to your destination. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us.

What we don't

No accounts, passwords, cookies, analytics, or ad trackers. Your IP address is used in memory to rate-limit sign-ups and is not written to our database or logs.

Who else is involved

Stripe (payments), Fly.io (hosting), and whichever service you chose for alerts (Discord or ntfy) receive what they need to do their part. Our checks against recreation.gov carry no information about you.

Keeping and deleting

A watch is kept until you unsubscribe or it ends. Every alert includes an unsubscribe link. To delete everything tied to a destination, write to [support email].
